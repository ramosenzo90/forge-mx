Forged was created for the rider who doesn't want to worry when on the track. Forged is a small shop, which allows for more attention to detail and a personal touch. This year I was diagnosed with OCD and felt so lost and overwhelmed. I kept my faith strong in Christ and used the blessings he has given me to enjoy hobbies and overcome obstacles in life, instead of focusing on my problems.

Jesus has saved me. He forged my problems into a gift to help me do what I enjoy. OCD has given me a heightened attention to detail and precision that helps me to do quality work you can trust in. Let's forge your ride into something more.
